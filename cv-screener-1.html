1. Fill the gaps (optional)

The AI finds what the job asks for that your CV does not show and drafts one line for each. Tap Yes only if it is true for you (edit the text and replace any [X] with your real detail). Tap No to skip it.

2. Choose a format and build

Nothing is invented: the tool only reorders and highlights what your CV already says. Claude rewrites without adding facts. Edit freely below.
